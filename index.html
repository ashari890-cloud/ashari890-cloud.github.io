My writing • A memoir

Trauma Didn’t Win

Born to Break Cycles

Trauma Didn’t Win: Born to Break Cycles

By Ashari Allen

A deeply personal memoir about growing up in survival mode and learning how childhood experiences can shape love, relationships, motherhood, and the person we become. With honesty and accountability, Ashari looks at the pain she endured, the choices she made, and the work of healing without pretending to be a perfect survivor.

This is a story about finding your voice, looking within, and choosing to break cycles rather than repeat them.

“Trauma didn't win. But you'll have to read the story to understand why.”
